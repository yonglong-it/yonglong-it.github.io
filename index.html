Yonglong Li

I am in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Xi’an Jiaotong University. My research focuses on classical and quantum information theory, with interests in sequential hypothesis testing, channel capacity, constrained coding, and the mathematics of data storage.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Hong Kong in 2016, supervised by Professor Guangyue Han. Previously, I held research positions at the National University of Singapore and the University of California, San Diego.

01 / Areas of interest

Research

Quantum information & hypothesis testing

Sequential quantum hypothesis testing, adaptive measurement strategies, and quantum channel discrimination.

Classical information theory & statistics

Sequential and composite hypothesis testing, error exponents, entropy rates, and stationary ergodic processes.

Channel capacity & data storage

Deletion and finite-state channels, constrained coding, and information-theoretic models of flash memory.
